# About Me
Hi 👋 i'm Daniel. I'm currently wrapping up my bachelor's degree from the University of Toronto where I'm specializing in computer science, and majoring in mathematics. At the moment, I'm working on [Courseography](https://github.com/Courseography/courseography) for the Department of Computer Science. Last term, I worked on [MarkUs](https://github.com/MarkUsProject/Markus). If you're a recruiter or potential employer, I'm most proficient in full-stack development with about 8 months of experience working in the field. I enjoy building Apple Applications and I've recently published one to the Apple App Store. Moreover, I like reverse engineering iOS apps. Its safe to say that my love for programming started from my interest in tinkering. Check out my [Personal Website](https://portfolio-race-chi.vercel.app/) if you're interested in learning more!
